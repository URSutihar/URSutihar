## Research & Scientific Computing

### [HodgeRank: Human and Machine Perceptual Judgments](https://github.com/URSutihar/hodgerank)

Built a computational topology pipeline to study consistent rankings and higher-order inconsistencies in perceptual comparison data.

- Processed and analyzed **4.5M+ comparisons**, with statistical validation and interactive visualization.
- Used controlled null-model experiments to investigate whether observed topological signals reflected perceptual structure or dataset topology.
- Developed tools for exploring Hodge decompositions and comparing human and machine decision patterns.

### Stochastic Ecosystem Simulation

Modeled generalist and specialist predator strategies across **20 prey species** under stochastic shocks. Combined logistic growth, Lotka–Volterra dynamics, and Monte Carlo experiments to estimate extinction probabilities and survival rates, with reproducible simulations and D3.js visualizations.

### Multi-Objective Routing

Implemented genetic algorithms, simulated annealing, and ant colony optimization for a routing problem across **1,102 U.S. cities**. Benchmarked solutions against Gurobi under budget, travel-time, and attraction-coverage constraints.
